The first PyCarinthia meetup. The goal is simple: meet the people in Carinthia who build with Python and AI, and shape the community together.

Expect a short opening talk, lightning introductions from whoever wants to share what they are working on, and an open discussion about topics, venues, formats, and what people actually want from a local community.

No talk prepared? No problem. Come and listen, ask questions, or just see who else is here.

Python, AI, data, web, automation, open source, research, hobby projects - all of it is welcome. The only prerequisite is curiosity.
