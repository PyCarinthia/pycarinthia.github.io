The second PyCarinthia meetup is set for Wednesday, 13 January 2027, at the [NEXUS Hub powered by Kärntner Sparkasse](https://www.sparkasse.at/kaernten/nexus), right next to the University of Klagenfurt.

Expect talks on Python, AI, and everything around them, followed by open discussion and drinks. The speakers and the RSVP link will be announced here soon.

## Good to know

Doors open at 17:30. Please arrive before 20:00: after that, the building can only be entered with a bank card.

After the talks, networking continues in the NEXUS Hub.

Just here to listen? Perfect. Come along, ask questions, or simply see who else is around.
