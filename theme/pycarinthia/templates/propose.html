What makes a good session

A good session teaches something concrete or starts a useful conversation. It does not have to be polished. A rough demo with a clear lesson is often more valuable than a rehearsed slide deck.

Topics that work well: a Python library you use daily, an AI tool or workflow, a production story (including what went wrong), a data project, an open source contribution, or anything you wish someone had explained to you earlier.

Formats: a 20-minute talk, a 5-minute lightning intro, a hands-on workshop, or an open discussion. If you are unsure, suggest it anyway and we will figure out the format together.
